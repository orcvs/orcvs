# 808

`808.orcvs` plays one bar of a beat on the Built-in Synth's 808-style kit, on channel `08`. The
web console opens it on a first visit, when it has no Source stored. It also opens with
**Help > Examples > 808**, or as `?example=808`. Press Space to start Playback.

```text
col  0         1
     012345678901
  0
  1  || kick ||
  2  ~*0104
  3    !~087FC201
  4
  5  || snare ||
  6    ~.0108
  7  .=0004
  8    !~0864D201
  9
 10  || hat ||
 11  ~*0102
 12    !~0840f201
```

Each instrument stands under its own label, so a phone shows all three.

## What each part does

| Cells | Function | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (0, 2) `~*0104` | Delay, rate `01`, modulus `04` | Bangs every fourth Tick, on each beat. |
| (2, 3) `!~087FC201` | Timed Play | The kick: Note `C2` at velocity `7F`, held one Tick. |
| (2, 6) `~.0108` | Clock, rate `01`, modulus `08` | Counts `00` to `07` once a Tick and writes the count into Equality's `left`. |
| (0, 7) `.=0004` | Equality | Bangs when the count is `04`: beats 2 and 4. The shipped `00` is overwritten on the first Tick. |
| (2, 8) `!~0864D201` | Timed Play | The snare: Note `D2` at velocity `64`. |
| (0, 11) `~*0102` | Delay, rate `01`, modulus `02` | Bangs every second Tick, on each eighth. |
| (2, 12) `!~0840f201` | Timed Play | The closed hat: Note `f2` (F#2) at velocity `40`. |

At the default 120 BPM a Tick is a sixteenth note, so a bar is sixteen Ticks. The kit picks its
instrument from a Note's pitch class and ignores its octave.

The Clock stands on its own row rather than nested in Equality's `left`: a nested Function still
writes its answer one row south of its anchor, which would land on the snare's Timed Play.

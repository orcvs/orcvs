# 04 — Decide whether a standalone operand literal is diagnosed

**Status:** resolved

A 2026-10-16 ruling would stop diagnosing a Number or Note pair where an Expression could start,
reversing ADR 0021 there. `docs/requirements/language.md` lists it under "Open decisions" (decisions
D4), and LANG-130 binds until an ADR lands. The ADR is already tracked by
`.scratch/source-diagnostics/issues/01-record-that-a-standalone-operand-literal-is-not-diagnosed.md`,
so this ticket adds nothing and is closed in its favour.

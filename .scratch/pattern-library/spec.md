# Patterns library

Design confirmed on 18 November 2026. The
[research and agreed decisions](../../docs/research/pattern-library.md)
record Q1–Q16, the prior art and four small checked Grids.

Publish Kombine as the first complete entry, then preset recall. Each entry
provides a playable Source File, a sibling guide, a copyable Region and one
musically meaningful scaling edit. Discover entries through Help → Examples
(Q5, confirmed). A library index beside the examples is carried from the
research note's section 1 and is not a confirmed decision. Q10 confirms a
diagram only for Kombine's Region; a diagram for every entry generalises it and
is likewise unconfirmed. Where present, a diagram explains the Region, runtime
write space, output pairs and wiring after placement.

## Delivery order

1. **01 — Publish Kombine with a worked Tracker transfer.** Establish the
   entry format and verify it by following the guide in the console.
2. **02 — Publish preset recall with independent slews.** Apply what the
   completed first entry teaches about copying, documentation and validation.

Ticket 02 depends on 01 as an agreed product sequence. Existing research
Grids provide small execution studies. The tickets require evidence from the
actual shipped examples, their scaling edits and the documented transfer.

## Scope

Kombine cycles three pitches against four velocities. Its guide shortens the
accent cycle to three steps and transfers the accent Region into Tracker's
closed hi-hat using absolute Tick with a division of two.

Preset recall begins with two presets for cutoff and resonance on the
Built-in Synth's acid voice. One editable selector redirects both targets
on the next Tick. Each control slews from its current value at its own
fixed Slew `amount`. Adding decay demonstrates growth. Preset 00 is established at
load, selector values wrap by preset count, Rewind retains Source values,
and reopening restores the initial settings.

The further survey candidates remain future design possibilities.

# 05 — Show the Cursor and Region on the Overview

**What to build:** The performer can find their Cursor and Region on the Overview when either is off screen. The Cursor's Cell and the Region are marked over the picture in their existing Theme colours, drawn as overlays so a Cursor move or Region change rebuilds no picture. See `../spec.md` (Overlays).

## Comments

Shipped in `console/src/console/overview.rs`. `show_overview` marks the Region and then the Cursor's Cell on the picture, from the Render Frame it already reads, with the painter clipped to the picture that `ShownOverview` keeps, straight after the image is painted. `Console::ui` draws the Source View's outline from that painter after the Source is presented, so the order is the spec's: picture, Region, Cursor, outline. Each mark is one `RectShape` over its Cells, one texel per Cell past the two-Cell margin, in the Theme's fills and borders: the Region in `region.background` with `region.border`, the Cursor with `cursor.border`, both stroked inside. Each border is its Theme width capped at a quarter of a texel: an inside stroke half a texel wide or wider fills a one-texel side with the border's colour, so at about a point per texel a one-Cell Cursor or a narrow Region would otherwise show as a solid block of its border and hide the Cell and the Region's tint. The Cursor's Cell is filled with `cell_texel`, the texel the picture draws for a Cell, asked at the Cursor's placement (`CursorPlacement::Cursor`, or `RegionCursor` inside a spanning Region) rather than `Plain`. So its background comes from `CellColours::colours`, the decision the Source paints the Cursor's Cell from: `cursor.background`, or `Theme::region_cursor_fill` inside a spanning Region, composited over `cell.background`, and with both cleared, as both built-ins leave them, the plain Cell base, with no Region tint, as the Source shows it. An occupied Cursor's Cell keeps its foreground texel, as the picture shows every occupied Cell. No key is added. A Region of one Cell is the Cursor's Cell and gets no mark of its own, as Paint treats it. The Cursor keeps its mark inside a spanning Region, where the Source drops the Cursor's outline for the Region's, so the Overview always shows where the Cursor is. The marks are not in the picture, so the cache key stays the revision, the Theme and the appearance. Nothing animates.

Tests: `the_overview_marks_the_region_and_the_cursor_between_the_picture_and_the_outline` in `console::tests` drives the shipped `Console` with a four-by-two Region whose Cursor is its far corner, and finds the Region mark and the Cursor mark at the rects predicted from the picture's rect, one texel per Cell plus the margin, in the Theme's fills and border colours with each border narrower than half a texel, the Cursor's empty Cell in the Grid background rather than the Region's tint, painted after the image and before the outline; after a select collapses the Region, the Cursor's Cell is the only mark. `the_overview_uploads_its_picture_only_when_the_source_or_appearance_changes` now also moves the Cursor and extends the Region and asserts neither uploads. `the_overview_fills_the_cursor_mark_as_the_source_fills_the_cursors_cell` loads a custom Theme that sets `cursor.background` and `region.cursor.background` apart and finds the Cursor mark in each in turn; `the_overview_fills_the_cursor_mark_with_the_cell_base_when_the_cursor_fills_are_cleared` sets only an opaque `cell.background` and finds the Cursor mark in it both inside a spanning Region and alone. `source_view_outlines` takes the Theme and picks the outline by its outline colour, since the Cursor mark is now a stroked rect on the picture too.

With this ticket every Overview ticket is resolved, so the spec is too.

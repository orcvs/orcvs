# Bang activation includes Self-Banging Functions

Bang is the two-Cell Atom `**`, and every Function has one canonical spelling. An ordinary root Expression is inert until Bang activation. A Self-Banging Function is the explicit exception in activation source: at its own Source-order turn it intrinsically receives Bang activation without creating a Source-resident `**`. This preserves one evaluation rule—an active root evaluates—while making autonomous motion visible in the Function spelling rather than in Function case.

For a Source-resident Bang anchored at `(x, y)`, the aligned cardinal root anchors are north `(x, y-1)`, south `(x, y+1)`, west `(x-2, y)`, and east `(x+2, y)`; only a complete root at one of those anchors activates. A Portal may instead deliver Bang directly to one root without overwriting its Function Cells. Activation recursively includes nested Functions. A Bang in the Source Snapshot plans removal at the current commit. A Bang generated during planning is written at that commit, participates in the next Source Snapshot, and is removed by the next commit. Direct delivery and intrinsic self-Bang are activation events, not stored glyphs.

Activation scheduling follows one row-major pass. The Interpreter visits each Expression root once at its anchor Position and tests whether it is active and unlocked. A Self-Banging Function receives intrinsic activation at that turn. A Bang delivered during the pass can activate a root whose turn is ahead, but no root whose turn has passed is revisited; multiple Bangs do not make one root evaluate twice. This visit order decides whether a root has a turn. Effects use ADR 0020's producer anchor and emission order, with later Cell effects winning independently under ADR 0004.

The Directional Bang Functions `*^`, `*v`, `*<`, and `*>` emit the matching root-only Self-Banging Function `^^`, `vv`, `<<`, or `>>`. For a producer at `(x, y)`, north emits at `(x, y-1)`, south at `(x,y+1)`, west at `(x-2,y)`, and east at `(x+2,y)`; every emitted Function has a two-Cell Span. The complete initial destination must be empty and inside the Grid or the producer diagnoses and emits nothing. Generated Functions first receive a turn from the next Source Snapshot.

At its turn, a Self-Banging Function tests only Cells newly entered by a one-Cell move. An empty destination plans one validated Portal bundle: spaces over the current Span, followed by its own spelling at the shifted destination. A blocked or out-of-Grid move instead replaces its current Span with `**`. Complete aligned root contact also directly delivers Bang activation; a later root evaluates at its turn, while an earlier root is not revisited. Partial Language Unit contact diagnoses and delivers nothing. Complete non-root contact adds no collision diagnostic. The committed `**` remains visible for its ordinary one-Tick Source life. Direction and cross-Tick state remain visible in Source.

Halt `*!` belongs in this decision because its effect is on activation itself: it withholds activation from a root rather than moving a value or changing an address. When Halt is active at its turn, it targets only the Expression root whose anchor Position is one row directly south. It locks that root before the later southward turn, so the complete target Expression contributes no effects. Halt does not change the target Source. An empty target is a no-op; an occupied non-root target diagnoses. Orcvs does not revisit a Halt whose turn has passed, and a suppressed Halt does not lock its own target.

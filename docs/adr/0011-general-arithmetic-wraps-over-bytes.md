# General arithmetic wraps over bytes

General Orcvs addition `+.`, subtraction `-.`, and multiplication `x.` wrap modulo 256 across the full `00`–`FF` Number range instead of saturating at its boundaries. Cyclic byte arithmetic fits hexadecimal tracker workflows and keeps sequences moving, while Functions for MIDI and other narrower domains define their own boundaries; symmetric absolute difference is the separate `|.` Function rather than the semantics of ordered subtraction. Division `/.` by zero has no cyclic interpretation, so it produces a diagnostic and no result rather than inventing a Number.

Notes retain their distinct type while participating in numeric Functions and wrap modulo 128 across the complete MIDI note range. Every result remains Source-round-trippable through two-Cell notation: uppercase pitch letters are naturals, lowercase pitch letters are sharps, `/` denotes the octave below zero, and decimal octave glyphs cover zero through nine, giving `C/` for MIDI `00` through `G9` for MIDI `7F`. Explicit Note-to-Number conversion is the operation that discards musical identity; ordinary numeric evaluation does not do so implicitly.

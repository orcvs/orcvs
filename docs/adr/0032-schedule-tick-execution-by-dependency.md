# Schedule Tick execution by dependency

Placement admission and its relationship to value readiness and overwrite ordering are amended by [ADR 0060](0060-value-inputs-wait-placement-tests-occupancy.md).

Status: accepted, partially superseded by [ADR 0034](0034-execute-against-live-typed-expressions.md) for partial and competing writes, failed spatial suppliers, nested delivery, and original-anchor replacement; [ADR 0061](0061-a-nested-function-returns-to-the-slot-it-occupies.md) for encoded Returns decoded by the receiving operand; [ADR 0062](0062-a-blank-operand-gives-a-blank-answer.md) for blank-result writes; [ADR 0063](0063-a-list-is-cells-in-a-claim-not-a-value.md) for Lists and removal of variable-width Sequence projection; and [ADR 0036](0036-reserve-result-cells-before-their-width-exists.md) for the earlier reservation decision. Supersedes ADR 0031 and the row-major execution, snapshot-only operand reads, and stored-Bang activation clauses of ADRs 0003, 0004, 0006, 0009, 0012, 0014, and 0020.

When Functions supply a Note and a neighboring Bang to MIDI during Tick T, that Note must play during T, including when a producer appears below its consumer. Parse the program, establish data and activation dependencies, order the operations, execute each scheduled root at most once, and publish the Tick outcome. Grid position breaks ties between independent operations; it does not override a dependency. A fixed upward Portal is an ordinary dependency, not delayed feedback.

Parsing owns initial Function identities, operand positions, nested connections, and contextual Bang validity. Under ADR 0034 the current Function signature interprets literal encodings; original-anchor replacement retains positions and connections rather than freezing the original literal types. Preserve that structure, including missing or invalid operand slots that current-Tick writes can supply. Nested Functions remain owned by their containing Expression and use the existing Evaluator once each. Their answers are encoded for Return and decoded by the receiving operand under ADR 0061; each nested Function also projects through its fixed Output Portal. Scheduling and current operand resolution stay behind the Source Tick interface; Playback receives the ordered musical commands for T.

A Function's Bang result activates neighboring roots during its production Tick. `**` is its transient visual representation; manual `**` is a no-op, and the displayed result does not activate again on T+1. Bang is a value, not a standalone Function. A `**` rejected in a typed operand is still invalid syntax and neither activates nor receives display cleanup. The initial implementation can discard valid prior Bang display before evaluation without recording pulse-age metadata.

Spatial writes preserve Cell encoding semantics: the receiving operand decodes the surviving encoding according to its current Function signature when consumed (ADR 0034). Whole and partial writes compose Cell-wise without intermediate decoding. Nested evaluation returns Source encodings decoded by each receiving operand under ADR 0061. A potential supplier must settle before its consumer, even when its result is absent; absence of Bang means no activation, not an unfinished dependency. Ordinary absence performs no write and preserves existing operand data; a blank inline operand instead answers blank and writes spaces under ADR 0062. ADR 0034 also permits a spatial consumer to use surviving characters after a supplier fails; a nested consumer requires a successful Return, whose encoding the receiver decodes under ADR 0061; a blank Return follows ADR 0062.

## Initial scope and consequences

The original implementation admitted fixed destinations and whole-slot scalar projections, rejecting competing writers, partial inputs and structural writes together with cycles. [ADR 0034](0034-execute-against-live-typed-expressions.md) revises those restrictions: partial and competing writes compose deterministically, values may suppress original computations and descendants, and Function replacement at an original anchor retains inputs and connections. A replacement's signature decodes retained literal characters. These revisions preserve cycle atomicity, fixed destinations and existing activation behavior. [ADR 0036](0036-reserve-result-cells-before-their-width-exists.md) lifts the restriction against variable-width Sequence projection: scheduling reserves the Cells a result can reach from what its Functions declare, so a Sequence uses the dependency edges a scalar does. Newly anchored code, arbitrary computed destinations, explicit delayed feedback, and replacements changing activation or output behavior remain deferred.

Preserve the Source/Playback separation and atomic Tick publication from ADR 0001. The first implementation retains the current Function-family activation policy—value roots calculate each Tick and terminal roots require a Bang—while making their ordering and Bang lifetime correct. This decision does not implement the broader directional movement, Jump, or Halt designs; those tickets must adopt the dependency model before adding their effects.

The bounded prototype demonstrated same-Tick C4 with producers above and below MIDI, the exact `Y-1` Bang Portal, a calculation chain, absence, conflicts, cycles, invalid operands, manual no-op, and no replay.

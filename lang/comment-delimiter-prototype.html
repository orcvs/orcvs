Comment delimiter walkthrough

Throwaway syntax prototype. It answers one question from .scratch/comment-delimiter/spec.md: which delimiter should open and close a Comment, now that the tracker frames each pattern row in one. The rows, the Reads and the diagnostics follow ADR 0035 and ADR 0074; only the delimiter spelling is hypothetical, and each model labels it. Diagnostic texts are the Parser's own (unknown function, expected a function) and the Timed Play's (expected a note).
